Chat cards

The bot says what it needs in a plain sentence, then offers the choices as full-width buttons stacked underneath. Tool activity and status are one grey line, not a card. A card never blocks the chat: you can keep typing and answer later. Most of groups 1 to 4 is what OpenMausBot has and CachiBot doesn’t yet.
